# RBI Digital Lending Directions
Jurisdiction: India | Version: 2025 | Effective date: 2025-01-01

## Explicit borrower consent
A borrower shall receive clear information and provide explicit, purpose-specific consent before personal data is collected or shared for a digital lending journey. Consent and its scope shall be recorded and revocable where applicable.

## Key Fact Statement
A Key Fact Statement (KFS) shall be presented and acknowledged before execution of the loan contract. Charges, repayment terms, and material loan conditions shall be disclosed in a clear, durable form.

## Disbursal and repayment
Loan disbursal and repayment shall flow directly between the borrower and the regulated entity's bank account, subject to applicable permitted arrangements. The system shall retain an auditable transaction trail.

## Cooling-off period
The digital lending journey shall disclose an applicable cooling-off or look-up period and support the corresponding borrower exit and repayment workflow.

## Data storage and access
Personal data shall be collected only as required for the stated lending purpose. Storage, access, and processing shall follow applicable regulatory and bank data-localization requirements, with controlled access and auditable deletion or retention handling.
