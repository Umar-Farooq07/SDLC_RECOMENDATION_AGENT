# RBI KYC Direction: Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP)
Jurisdiction: India | Version: 2025 | Effective date: 2025-01-01

## V-CIP process controls
The regulated entity shall conduct V-CIP using a secure, live, informed-consent interaction with a trained official. The official shall confirm the customer's identity and record the process in an auditable manner.

## Live location and liveness
The process shall capture live location and timestamp evidence and use liveness controls to reduce spoofing, replay, and impersonation risk. Failed liveness or interrupted connectivity shall route the case to a controlled exception and review process.

## PAN and Officially Valid Document
PAN shall be verified through an authorized issuer or verification service such as NSDL or UTIITSL. An Officially Valid Document (OVD) or permitted equivalent shall be verified and associated with the customer record before account activation.

## Video and audit retention
V-CIP video and associated audit logs shall be encrypted and retained for at least five years, or longer where another applicable statutory obligation requires it. Access shall be restricted, logged, and available for authorized audit.

## Concurrent audit before activation
The V-CIP record shall be subjected to concurrent audit. Account activation shall not occur until the required verification and audit controls have been completed, with exceptions routed for authorized resolution.
