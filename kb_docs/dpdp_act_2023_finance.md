# Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: Financial Services Control Notes
Jurisdiction: India | Version: 2025 | Effective date: 2023-08-11

## Itemized notice
Before or at the time of consent, the data fiduciary shall provide a notice describing the personal data and the purpose for which it is processed, together with a clear way to exercise applicable rights and contact the organization.

## Purpose-limited consent
Consent shall be free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous, expressed through clear affirmative action. Personal data shall be processed for the specified purpose and limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

## Erasure and statutory retention
When consent is withdrawn or the purpose is no longer served, personal data shall be erased unless retention is required to comply with law. Financial institutions shall document the applicable statutory retention basis and restrict retained data to that basis.

## Security safeguards and breach notification
Reasonable security safeguards shall protect personal data. A personal data breach shall be assessed, documented, and notified to the Data Protection Board and affected individuals as required by applicable rules and timelines.

## Accountability
The organization shall maintain evidence of notices, consent, processing purposes, access, security controls, retention decisions, and rights requests.
